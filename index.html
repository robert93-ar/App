    🚨 Urgent / Symptoms Red / Amber
    Blue Balls (Epididymal Hypertension)

    The Cause: Vasocongestion. Blood enters arteries faster than it leaves veins. Retention keeps this blood trapped, causing a dull ache.

    Treatment Protocol

    1. Do NOT Edge: Touching makes it worse.
    2. Fire & Ice: Warm bath (vasodilation) then cold rinse (vasoconstriction) to pump blood out.
    3. Support: Wear supportive briefs (not boxers) for 1-2 hours.
    4. Movement: 20 Air Squats.
    The Donut (Lymphatic Edema)

    The Look: A puffy, fluid-filled ring below the glans.

    The Cause: Pumping too long or high pressure trapped lymph fluid (not blood).

    Treatment Protocol

    1. The “Milking” Massage: Gently stroke from the glans down toward the base to push fluid back in.
    2. Warm Compress: Apply a warm washcloth for 5 mins.
    3. Rest: Usually absorbs in 2-12 hours. Do not pump again until gone.
    Petechiae (Red Dots)

    The Cause: Capillaries (tiny blood vessels) burst under vacuum.

    Treatment Protocol

    1. Vitamin K / Arnica: Apply a cream containing Arnica or Vitamin K.
    2. Total Rest: 48-72 hours. No exceptions.
    📉 The "Hard Flaccid" Guide

    The Signs: Penis feels rigid/rubbery while soft. Glans is cold. Scrotum is tight/high.

    The Cause: Your pelvic floor is in a protective spasm (chronic clench).

    The Cure (Reverse Kegels)

    • Do NOT do regular Kegels (squeezing).
    • The Move: Inhale and gently push your perineum out (like starting to pee). Hold for 5s.
    • The Stretch: Happy Baby Pose or Deep Squat for 2 minutes.

    Manual Release (Trigger Point)

    1. Lie on back. Knees bent.
    2. Place thumb on perineum (spot between sack and anus).
    3. Hunt for “ropes” or tight bands.
    4. Hold pressure (mild discomfort) for 30-60s until it melts.
