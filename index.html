    Blue Balls (Pelvic Ache)

    What is it? Epididymal hypertension. A deep ache in the testicles or perineum caused by prolonged blood flow without release (vasocongestion). Common in retention/stamina training.

    Protocol

    • Do not force ejaculation just to fix it (this breaks the retention mindset).
    • Pelvic softening: Lie down. Inhale 4s, exhale 8s. Visualize the pelvic floor dropping.
    • Cold therapy: Use the Urge SOS cold rinse to constrict vessels and flush blood out.
    • Movement: 20 air squats to redirect blood to large leg muscles.
    Red Dots (Petechiae)

    What is it? Tiny red or purple dots on the skin surface. These are burst capillaries (micro-bruises).

    Cause: Vacuum pressure was too high or increased too fast.

    Protocol (Strict)

    • Stop pumping: Do not pump again until 100% healed (usually 2-4 days).
    • Lighter pressure: Next time, reduce vacuum by 50%.
    • Note: If spots are large (bruises) or dark purple, see a doctor.
    The Cold Signal (Numbness)

    The Feeling: Cold head, pins-and-needles, or loss of sensation.

    Meaning: NERVE COMPRESSION. This is the #1 signal to stop.

    Action

    • Abort session immediately. Remove all devices.
    • Restore blood flow with warmth/massage.
    • Do not train again for 48 hours.
    Signs of Progress (Green Flags)
    • The warmth: Heavy, relaxed warmth in the genitals (not tight/bursting).
    • Morning wood: Waking up with erections 5+ days a week.
    • The disconnect: Feeling physically aroused (hard) but mentally calm (heart rate slow).
